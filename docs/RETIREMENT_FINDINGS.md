## 🔍 Strategy Comparisons (Across All Tests)

1.  **Current (60/40 US/Intl):** Often outperformed theoretical "pre-set" strategies in success rate for growth-oriented profiles.
2.  **Risk-Matched:** Showed higher volatility but often maintained the median capital better than more conservative tilts.
3.  **Income-Focused:** Consistently the lowest success rate for young accumulators, as the lower expected returns (4.7%) failed to outpace inflation and withdrawal needs.
4.  **US-Focused:** Generally slightly more conservative than the 60/40 current allocation in these tests, leading to lower final medians.
